What is AsterDEX

What AsterDEX is

A non-custodial exchange on BNB Chain

AsterDEX is a decentralized exchange on BNB Chain that offers spot trading and perpetual contracts, with trades settled on-chain. Instead of depositing funds with a company, you trade from your own wallet and keep control of your keys.

The ASTER token

ASTER is the token associated with the protocol. Holding it is separate from trading: you do not need it to open a spot or perpetual position, and its price can move independently of the markets you trade.

Spot vs perpetual

Spot vs perpetual trading

Both markets quote a price, but they work very differently. Knowing which one you are in tells you what you own and what can go wrong.

In practice

A spot trade swaps one token for another and the result sits in your wallet. A perpetual lets you take a long or short view with margin, so losses can reach the collateral you posted.

How to trade

How to trade on AsterDEX

Before the first order

Make sure your wallet is on BNB Chain and holds BNB for gas plus the asset you want to trade or post as margin.

Step by step

  1. Connect your wallet and confirm the network is BNB Chain.
  2. Pick a spot pair or a perpetual market.
  3. Enter the size and, for a perpetual, check the margin, leverage and liquidation price.
  4. Review the order, the trading fee and the estimated gas.
  5. Sign the transaction; it settles on-chain and your balance or position updates.

Start with a small size until you are comfortable with how margin and funding behave.

Fees & gas

What trading costs

Gas

Transactions on BNB Chain are paid in BNB, so keep a small BNB balance in the wallet you trade from.

Trading fees

Spot and perpetual orders carry a trading fee, and spot swaps also have price impact and slippage. The exact fee for a market is shown in the interface before you sign.

Funding on perpetuals

Perpetual positions exchange a funding payment between longs and shorts. Depending on the market and your side, it can add to or reduce the cost of holding a position.

Security

Staying safe

Non-custodial by design

You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction yourself. This interface never holds your funds or private keys.

Before you trade

  • Leverage magnifies losses; a perpetual can be liquidated and lose the posted margin.
  • Keep leverage low and watch the liquidation price, especially in fast markets.
  • Verify token contract addresses; lookalike tokens are common.
  • Smart contracts can contain bugs, so risk is never zero.
FAQ

AsterDEX FAQ

What is AsterDEX?

AsterDEX is a decentralized exchange on BNB Chain with spot and perpetual markets. Trades settle on-chain, and you trade from your own wallet instead of depositing funds with a company.

What is the difference between spot and perpetual markets?

Spot trading swaps tokens you then own. A perpetual is a contract that tracks a price with no expiry and can use leverage, so it adds margin, funding payments and liquidation risk.

Do I need the ASTER token to trade?

No. ASTER is a separate token, and trading spot or perpetual markets does not require holding it. You need the asset you trade or post as margin, plus BNB for gas.

What happens if my position is liquidated?

If the price moves against a leveraged perpetual until your margin no longer covers the position, it is closed and the posted margin can be lost. Lower leverage keeps the liquidation price further from the market.

What is a funding rate?

It is a periodic payment between long and short holders that keeps a perpetual's price close to the underlying market. Depending on your side and the market, you may pay it or receive it.

Does this interface hold my funds?

No. It is non-custodial: you connect your own wallet and sign each transaction. There is no account to open, and the interface never takes custody of your keys.
